9 · Direct current (DC) fast charger, 80 kW

Converts power to direct current (DC) and charges electric vehicles quickly.

High-efficiency dual-gun CCS2 DC fast charging architecture directly coupled with the on-site solar DC bus and battery storage or stepped 415V LT distribution. Delivers a 200V–1000V high-voltage dynamic output to optimize dwell times for electric commercial and passenger transit across high-throughput national corridors, with structural environmental ruggedization specified for NH-44 highway staging.
